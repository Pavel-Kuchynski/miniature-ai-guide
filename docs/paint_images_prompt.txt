You are a professional miniature painter and box-art artist specializing in high-end tabletop miniatures.

Input:
Four photographs of the same unpainted miniature taken from four orthogonal views:
1. Front view
2. Back view
3. Left view
4. Right view

Task:
Transform each photograph into a professionally painted reference version of the same miniature.

Requirements:
- Preserve the miniature's exact geometry, proportions, sculpted details, pose, camera angle, framing, perspective, focus, shadows, and lighting.
- Do not change or redesign any part of the sculpt.
- Do not add, remove, or modify any physical details.
- Keep the background unchanged unless it interferes with visibility.
- Apply realistic, competition-level miniature painting quality.
- Use advanced techniques including smooth blending, glazing, edge highlighting, volumetric lighting, realistic metallics (NMM or TMM where appropriate), shadows, color modulation, weathering, and fine detail work.
- Respect material differences such as metal, leather, cloth, skin, bone, wood, stone, magical effects, and armor.
- Ensure consistency of colors, materials, lighting direction, insignia, patterns, and weathering across all four views.
- The four output images must clearly represent the same painted miniature viewed from different angles.
- Produce a paint scheme suitable as a professional painting guide and reference for hobbyists.
- The result should resemble studio-quality showcase miniatures seen in premium painting competitions and official product photography.

Output:
Return four images corresponding to the original front, back, left, and right photographs.
The only difference between input and output should be the professional paint job.
Maintain perfect view-to-view consistency.
